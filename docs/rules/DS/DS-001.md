# Specification Name

Market Data Specification

---

# Status

Approved

---

# Purpose

Define the shared market data conventions used throughout the RMR-OS project.

This specification establishes the common interpretation of candles, timestamps, trading days and price values.

All business rules operating on market data shall conform to this specification.

---

# Daily Candle Convention

A Daily candle represents one completed trading day.

The timestamp carried by a Daily candle uniquely identifies that completed Daily candle.

A Daily candle becomes available only after it has completely closed.

No business rule shall use an in-progress Daily candle unless explicitly permitted by another approved specification.

---

# Calendar Day Convention

A calendar day is determined using the timestamp carried by the Daily candle.

Business rules using calendar-day lookback windows shall determine window membership using the Daily candle timestamp.

When a specification references the latest completed Daily candle, it refers to the completed Daily candle having the newest timestamp.

---

# Timestamp Convention

All candle timestamps shall be unique.

Candle timestamps shall be strictly chronological.

Duplicate timestamps are invalid.

RMR-OS uses the timestamps supplied by the configured market data source.

RMR-OS performs no timezone conversion.

All business rules interpret Daily candles exactly as supplied by the configured market data source.

---

# Price Convention

All price values use the precision supplied by the configured market data source.

Business rules shall compare price values exactly as defined by their individual specifications.

No implicit price rounding shall be performed.

---

# Candle Structure

Every candle consists of:

- Timestamp
- Open
- High
- Low
- Close

Additional fields may exist but are outside the scope of this specification.

---

# Responsibilities

DS-001 defines:

- Daily candle completion
- Daily candle timestamp convention
- Calendar-day interpretation
- Timestamp requirements
- Shared candle structure
- Shared price conventions

---

# Limitations

DS-001 does not define:

- Fair Value Gap detection
- Touch detection
- Target selection
- Trade execution
- Money management
- Lower-timeframe analysis
